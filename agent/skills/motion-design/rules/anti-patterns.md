# Review symptoms

Use after inspecting a rendered proof. Diagnose the observed weakness before
changing a parameter. A named effect, palette or compositional style is not a
failure by itself.

## Close findings with evidence

Record the expected event or relationship, the observed frames/time range, and
the decision. For a mismatch, trace the inputs that own the behavior before
changing it: child timing, parent transforms, shared geometry or the scene budget.
Inspect the changed interval and its dependent neighbors again.

An exception needs a bounded range, a purpose grounded in the brief or selected
direction, and a visible result that preserves the important information. A word
passing through its own reveal mask can be deliberate; two unrelated phrases
remaining readable over each other do not become a successful replacement merely
because the report calls their overlap informational. Fix contradictions between
the stated plan and actual output. If the creative choice changes, update the
plan and inspect that new choice rather than relabeling the old finding.

Example diagnosis: expected the slot to clear before phrase B; observed A's last
words under B at frames 190–195; found that the plan omitted the last members'
exit stagger. Derive B from the complete exit, inspect both sides of the new
boundary and record the actual verification.
Report what was actually inspected; generated stills alone do not establish
continuous playback quality or an audio audition.

## Diagnose the rendered symptom

| Observed symptom | Investigate | Targeted correction | Counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unclear subject | Competing contrast, size and motion | Establish the intended focus and subordinate unrelated content | A single centered phrase can be sufficient |
| Attractive UI with no clear event | Missing target, activation or response | Connect the action to its visible result | A type announcement need not pretend to be an interactive demo |
| Transition seems broken | Object ownership, mismatched geometry, timing or an unexplained extra cut | Inspect the handoff and derive shared anchors | A deliberate hard cut needs no persistent object |
| Next phrase overlaps the previous tail | Last-child exit, stagger and the shared reading position | Derive group completion and the following event from the member schedule | A masked word replacement can deliberately share a handoff interval |
| Result disappears before it reads | Occlusion, transition overlap, wrong crop or insufficient time | Budget the actual readable interval | A fast accent can be short when no detailed reading is intended |
| Everything feels restless | Unmotivated camera/scale drift, concurrent gestures | Let completed content rest and keep motion with a role | Environmental footage may continue moving throughout |
| Every shot looks interchangeable | Generic placeholders or repeated staging without progression | Show specific content and vary staging where the meaning changes | Repeated typography can establish a coherent rhythm |
| Text shakes or glyphs change | Font readiness, layout shifts, transforms or wall-clock CSS | Reproduce on consecutive rendered frames and apply a scoped technical fix | Required vertical text motion is not automatically forbidden |
| Fine detail disappears | Source resolution, crop, contrast or encoding | Inspect the delivery encode at normal viewing size | Decorative detail need not meet a fixed opacity quota |
| Audio and picture feel separate | Visual peak mapped to onset, wrong cue or unreviewed final mix | Align the intended event and listen to the exported fragment | Many valid edits do not cut on every beat |
| Arrival hits an invisible wall | Per-frame deltas over the last frames before rest; a clamped underdamped spring stops dead, then pulls back ([springs](springs.md)) | Choose the stop: a cushioned settle, or a hard accent on a beat | Words snapping in on the beat in a brutalist direction |
| Subject jitters against a smooth background | Layers on different cadences: a stepped layer over a smooth pan, 24 fps footage in a 30 fps video, rounded time | Give layers that move against each other one cadence (`interpolate`'s `posterize` on both or neither) | A stepped character over a locked background |

Report technical coverage separately from the creative comparison. For the latter,
cite the rendered passage and the reference relationship or declared direction
being assessed. A clean report measures only the checks it ran; it does not prove
that the chosen sequence develops the message or uses its time well.
