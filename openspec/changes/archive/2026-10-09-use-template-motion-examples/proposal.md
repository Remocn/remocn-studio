## Why

The generation instructions explain motion principles, but do not route agents to the complete remocn templates that demonstrate them. The user approved using the whole template catalog as motion examples so routine choreography remains the agent's responsibility.

## What Changes

- Add a task-oriented index and source-grounded studies for all five current templates inside `motion-design`.
- Route new video direction and motion work through the index, loading only relevant studies and actual source passages.
- Describe how to preserve attention, geometry and property timing while adapting copy, assets, brand, duration and format.
- Require the existing motion proof and review to record the selected example, adaptations and observed differences; no new pipeline stage.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `agent/knowledge`: the existing motion skill supplies guidance for selecting and adapting examples across the template catalog.
- `agent/pipeline`: existing motion and review instructions carry example selection and adaptation evidence.

## Impact

Agent-owned Markdown, skill-aware conventions and pipeline instruction strings change. Existing knowledge delivery and tools supply access to local or published sources. No IPC, sidecar runtime, Rust, webview, schema, entitlement or dependency changes.

## Non-goals

- Rebuilding templates or installing every template into every generated video.
- Shipping another source/media snapshot or changing the bundled component library.
- Automatic visual scoring, new playback tools or a guaranteed creative-quality threshold.
- Imposing one template's brand or narrative on every brief.
